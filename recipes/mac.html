Buffalo Mac and Cheese

A soft cheesy pasta with a little kick

Ingredients:

  • 20oz Cheese
  • 10oz Chicken
  • 30oz Mac Noodles
  • 15oz Buffalo Sauce
  • 5oz Croutons

Steps:

  1. Heat the stove top and get a pot of water
  2. Put noodles in the pot which should be boiling
  3. drain the water from the noodles
  4. Melt the cheese with your prefered method and pour it in the pot
  5. Dump the buffalo sauce in and top the Mac with croutons
  6. Scoop mac into a bowl and enjoy
