Grilled Steak

A perfectly seared steak topped with argentian butter and basil.

Ingredients:

  • 1lb Steak
  • 10oz Butter
  • Packet of Seasoning
  • Basil

Steps

  1. Heat the stove top and get a pan
  2. Season steak in seperate bowl
  3. Put butter in pan followed by the steak
  4. Cook steak for 3 minutes on each side
  5. Put steak on a plate and enjoy
