Grilled Shrimp Skewers

A perfectly seasoned yummy tender ocean tasting delight

Ingredients:

  • 5lbs shrimp
  • 10oz Butter
  • 10oz salt
  • 10oz Lemon Juice

Steps:

  1. Heat the grill
  2. Put all shrimp in an empty bowl
  3. Pu the salt and lemon juice in the bowl and mix them around
  4. But butter in the pan on the stove and put the shrimp in
  5. Cook the shrimp til they are to prefered texture
  6. Throw shrimp on skewers and enjoy
